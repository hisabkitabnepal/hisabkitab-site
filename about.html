About

Built for businesses that keep real books

Hisab Kitab started from a simple observation: most small and mid-sized businesses in Nepal don't fall behind on tax and compliance because they're careless — they fall behind because nobody translated IRD's process into something they could actually follow.

What we do differently

We work hands-on with owners across trading, manufacturing and retail — not just filing paperwork, but actually explaining what each notice, deadline and registration step means for your business, in plain English or Nepali.

That means fewer surprises: you know what's due, what it costs, and what happens if you miss it, before it becomes a problem.

How we work

  • Direct WhatsApp access — no call centres, no ticket queues
  • Flat pricing agreed before any work starts
  • Deadlines tracked on our side, not left to you to remember
  • Documents and filings you can review anytime, not just at year-end
