About Hisab Kitab

A Kathmandu-based team helping businesses across Nepal stay compliant, get registered, and stay audit-ready — without the runaround.

Our story

Hisab Kitab was started to solve a problem most business owners in Nepal run into sooner or later: tax compliance, registration, and audit work are scattered across different offices, forms, and departments, and it's easy to lose track of what's actually needed.

Rather than reviewing paperwork after the fact or handling only one piece of the process, we manage the entire thing — from checking your PAN/VAT status and filing overdue returns, to registering a new business from scratch, to preparing for a statutory audit, to closing a business down properly. One point of contact, from first call to final document.

We work with sole proprietorships, partnerships, and private limited companies, in Kathmandu and across the rest of Nepal, in businesses that are just getting started and ones that have been operating for years but have fallen behind on filings.

Credentials & approach

  • Our filing and audit approach follows ICAN-aligned standards and current Inland Revenue Department (IRD) procedures.
  • We handle registration through the relevant local body — ward office, municipality, or the Office of the Company Registrar (OCR) — depending on your business structure.
