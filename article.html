Documents required to register a private limited company in Nepal

Published Aug 10, 2026 · Hisab Kitab Team · 6 min read

On this page

  • MOA and AOA

  • Common delays

Before you start

Registering a private limited company in Nepal goes through the Office of the Company Registrar (OCR), and having the right documents ready before you begin makes the difference between a smooth few days and weeks of back-and-forth. This guide lists what's typically needed, though your exact requirements can vary slightly depending on the number of promoters and the nature of the business.

Core documents

At minimum, most private limited registrations require the following from each promoter or director:

  • Citizenship certificate (or passport for foreign promoters)
  • Two recent passport-size photographs
  • PAN details, if already registered individually
  • Proof of registered office address, such as a rental agreement or land ownership document

You'll also need to decide on a company name in advance and check its availability with OCR before filing, since a name that's already taken or too similar to an existing one will be rejected.

Memorandum and Articles of Association

Every private limited company needs a Memorandum of Association (MOA) and Articles of Association (AOA). The MOA sets out the company's objectives, authorised capital, and the shareholding of each promoter. The AOA sets the internal rules for how the company will be run — meetings, share transfers, and director responsibilities. Both documents need to be drafted carefully, since vague or overly broad objectives clauses are a common reason for delay during OCR review.

After registration

Once OCR issues the certificate of incorporation, registration isn't quite finished. Most companies still need to:

  • Register for PAN, and VAT if applicable, with the Inland Revenue Department
  • Register with the local ward office where the business operates
  • Open a company bank account using the incorporation certificate and PAN

Depending on the industry, additional sector-specific licenses may also be required before you can begin operating.

Common delays to avoid

The most frequent causes of delay we see are: a proposed name that isn't available, incomplete or inconsistent promoter documents, and an MOA objectives clause that doesn't clearly match the intended business activity. Reviewing these three areas closely before filing avoids the majority of back-and-forth with OCR.
