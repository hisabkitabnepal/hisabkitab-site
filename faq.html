Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to the questions we hear most often, grouped by topic.

Registration

A sole proprietorship can often be registered within about a week once documents are ready. A private limited company through the Office of the Company Registrar typically takes longer, depending on document readiness and office processing times. We give you a realistic estimate after reviewing your specific case.

At minimum you'll need citizenship documents for owners or partners, passport-size photos, a proposed business name, and proof of your business address. Private limited companies also need MOA and AOA drafts. Our document checklist tool on the Notices & Guides page walks through this in detail.

Filing

A suspended PAN usually means overdue returns or an unresolved notice from IRD. We identify the cause, clear the outstanding filings, and submit a reactivation request.

IRD assigns each business to a Large or Small Taxpayer Office based on turnover and business type. The office affects where returns are filed and, in some cases, how often. We handle filing correctly regardless of which office you're assigned to.

From time to time, government bodies introduce temporary schemes that reduce or waive penalties on overdue filings or unregistered businesses that come forward to formalize, usually with a fixed deadline. We track these schemes and let clients know when one applies to their situation.

Audit

Yes. We coordinate statutory annual audits, including pre-audit bookkeeping cleanup, reconciliations, and preparing supporting documents for the auditor.

Closure

Yes. Outstanding filings and dues are typically addressed as part of the closure process itself, rather than as a separate step beforehand.

General

No. While we're based in Kathmandu, much of our process can be handled remotely over calls, WhatsApp, and shared documents, so we work with businesses across Nepal.
