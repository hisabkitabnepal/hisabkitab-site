Service 03

Business Registration

PAN, VAT, company and ward-level registration, handled end to end.

What's included

  • PAN and VAT registration with IRD
  • Company registration with the Office of the Company Registrar
  • Ward office registration and local-level business permits
  • PAN-to-company or individual-to-company conversions
  • Document preparation: MOA/AOA, application letters, authorisations

Who this is for

New businesses starting from scratch, unregistered businesses looking to formalise (especially ahead of fine-waiver deadlines), or existing businesses restructuring their registration.
