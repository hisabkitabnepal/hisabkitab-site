Tax Filing & Compliance

Keeping your PAN/VAT status current and your returns filed on time, every time.

What it covers

  • PAN and VAT registration status checks and corrections
  • Monthly and trimester VAT return filing
  • TDS deduction, deposit, and D-01/D-02 return filing
  • Annual income tax return preparation and filing
  • Reactivating suspended PANs

Who it's for

  • Businesses currently filing but wanting fewer errors and less stress each period
  • Businesses behind on one or more filings and unsure how to catch up
  • Newly registered businesses filing for the first time
  • Both Large and Small Taxpayer Office jurisdictions

Typical process

1

Status review

We check your current PAN/VAT status and outstanding filings.

2

Document collection

You share sales/purchase records; we tell you exactly what's missing.

3

Filing

Returns are prepared and filed with IRD, and you receive copies.

4

Ongoing reminders

We flag upcoming deadlines so future filings stay on time.

What happens if my PAN is suspended?

A suspended PAN usually means overdue returns or an unresolved notice. We identify the cause, clear the outstanding items, and file a reactivation request with IRD.

What's the difference between Large and Small Taxpayer Office?

IRD assigns businesses to a Large or Small Taxpayer Office based on turnover and business type; the office affects where filings are submitted and, in some cases, filing frequency. We handle filing correctly for either.

Business Registration

Getting your business legally set up, under the right structure, from the start.

What it covers

  • Sole proprietorship registration at the ward/municipal office
  • Partnership firm registration and deed drafting
  • Private limited company registration through the Office of the Company Registrar (OCR)
  • PAN/VAT registration following incorporation
  • MOA/AOA drafting for private limited companies

Who it's for

  • First-time entrepreneurs unsure which structure fits their business
  • Founders who already know their structure and just need it filed correctly
  • Businesses formalising an operation that's been running informally

Typical process & timeline

1

Structure & name

We help confirm the right structure and check name availability. (1–2 days)

2

Document preparation

MOA/AOA, deeds, or forms are drafted for your signature. (2–4 days)

3

Filing & approval

We submit to the relevant office and follow up until approved. (Varies by office)

4

PAN/VAT & handover

We register PAN/VAT and hand over your complete registration set.

How long does registration usually take?

A sole proprietorship can often be registered within about a week; a private limited company through OCR typically takes longer depending on document readiness and office processing times. We give you a realistic estimate after reviewing your situation.

I haven't decided on a business structure yet — can you still help?

Yes. Many clients come to us before deciding. We walk through the trade-offs between sole proprietorship, partnership, and private limited based on your plans.

Audit

Getting your records organised and audit-ready, and managing the audit itself.

What it covers

  • Statutory annual audit coordination
  • Pre-audit bookkeeping review and cleanup
  • Internal audits for operational or compliance purposes
  • Preparing supporting schedules and reconciliations

Who it's for

  • Private limited companies with a statutory annual audit requirement
  • Businesses whose records aren't audit-ready yet
  • Owners who want an internal audit before a lender or investor review

Typical process

1

Records review

We assess your books and flag gaps before the audit begins.

2

Cleanup & reconciliation

Outstanding entries, reconciliations, and schedules are completed.

3

Audit coordination

We coordinate with the auditor and prepare all supporting documents.

4

Report & filing

The final audit report is issued and filed with the relevant office.

My books aren't in great shape — is that a problem?

It's a common starting point. We usually spend time cleaning up and reconciling records before the audit itself begins.

Do you handle both statutory and internal audits?

Yes — statutory annual audits for compliance, and internal audits for your own operational or financial review.

Business Closure

Closing a business properly, so it doesn't come back to cost you later.

What it covers

  • Clearing outstanding tax filings before closure
  • PAN/VAT deregistration
  • Formal closure filings with the relevant registering office
  • Final tax clearance certificate

Who it's for

  • Owners winding down a business that's no longer operating
  • Businesses with unfiled returns who need to clear those before closing
  • Owners who assumed a dormant business closes itself and need to fix the paper trail

Typical process

1

Status check

We review outstanding filings, dues, and the business's current status.

2

Clear outstanding items

Any pending returns or dues are filed and settled first.

3

Deregistration filing

We file for PAN/VAT deregistration and formal closure.

4

Clearance certificate

You receive final documentation confirming the closure.

I stopped operating years ago but never closed it officially — is that a problem?

It can mean unfiled returns and accumulating fines even though the business is inactive. We help assess the situation and, where applicable, look into available fine-waiver schemes before closing it out.

Can you close a business that has some unpaid dues?

Yes — outstanding filings and dues are usually addressed as part of the closure process itself, not a separate step beforehand.
